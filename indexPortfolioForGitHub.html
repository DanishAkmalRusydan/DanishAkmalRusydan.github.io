Churn Intelligence

CRISP-DMRuns fully localPDPA 2010EN · BM

A predictive member-retention platform for Klang Valley gyms

Built for membership-based fitness SMEs that lose members quietly. It runs on an ordinary laptop, scores every member each night and turns each risk into a clear, prioritised action for staff.

The problem and the approach

The problem

Small gyms rarely see a member leaving until the cancellation arrives. They lack data teams, and generic discounts leak money to members who would have stayed anyway.

The approach

Predict risk early from behaviour, explain it in plain language, act through the staff the gym already has, and then prove what actually worked with a held-out control group.

How it works

An end-to-end pipeline, from simulated behaviour to a measured outcome.

Simulate

A behaviour-driven gym of 2,500 members. Parameters were frozen before any model was trained.

Engineer

Compares each member's last 14 days with the 30 days before.

Model

Four models compared with SMOTE, cross-validation and grid search.

Explain

SHAP reasons in plain English and Bahasa Malaysia.

Act

A playbook turns risk tiers into tasks for front desk, trainers and admin.

Measure

Closed-loop attribution against a random control group.

Simulated signals include check-ins, payments, class bookings, app logins, freeze and cancellation enquiries, contracts and Malaysian holidays.

Results

Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest and XGBoost were compared. A calibrated Random Forest was selected.

Why these numbers are honest

The 0.808 ceiling is the best any model could score, because the simulator itself is noisy. The live score of 0.776 comes close to that limit.

Because the simulator's settings were frozen before training, the model could not be tuned to fit them.

Results are on synthetic data calibrated to the Klang Valley fitness context.

What the system does

Why? and what-if

Every decision has an explanation and shows what would lower the member's risk.

Retention playbook

WhatsApp nudges, PT check-ins, freeze offers, renewal perks, and Gmail offers whose voucher is chosen for the best expected value.

Closed-loop attribution

Measures real uplift, coupon leakage and customer value protected against a control group.

Role-based dashboard

A Flask dashboard with an analytics page that applies five analytics methods to each risk reason.

Festive-season advisory

Flags risk that is "likely seasonal" around Malaysian holidays.

Self-monitoring MLOps

Tracks its own live performance and retrains a challenger model monthly for the owner to approve.

PDPA toolkit

Data export, anonymisation, consent, retention policy and an audit log.

Owner reporting

A monthly PDF and Excel report plus a daily digest email.

Live demo mode

A simulator streams check-ins into the system, so it can be shown working day by day.
